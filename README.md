# Aurelien Brentraus

**Filmmaker, entrepreneur and vibecoder.** Founder of [BRECinema](https://brecinema.com) and Brentrausfilms Inc., Palo Alto.

I make films, and I build the software I wish I had as a filmmaker. My work sits where storytelling, AI and finance meet: I design products end to end, from the idea and the user journey to the architecture, the infrastructure and the launch.

## What I am building

| Venture | What it is |
|---|---|
| **BRECinema** | An AI film-making studio for filmmakers. Bring a script or an idea, plan it scene by scene, generate shots that keep continuity, then edit and export a finished film. [brecinema.com](https://brecinema.com) |
| **Story Studio** | Story-to-video production on local GPUs: scripts become shot lists, shots render through ComfyUI, the film assembles itself. |
| **OpenForge AI** | A self-hosted, multi-model AI inference platform with an OpenAI-compatible API and cost-aware routing. |
| **Who Is Trading** | A multi-agent trading desk for US stocks and crypto: AI research, a market-regime model, risk gates and paper-first execution. |
| **QuantumAlpha** | A browser-based trading workstation for technical analysis, backtesting and paper trading. |
| **BitLooper** | A post-quantum proof-of-stake payment blockchain with theft-protected accounts. |
| **Learning Lab** | A K-12 and university learning platform with narrated lessons, notebooks and an AI tutor. |
| **Restaurant Commerce OS** | Direct ordering, kitchen display and delivery for restaurants, without marketplace commissions. |

## How I work

- **Product first.** Every feature has to save a real person a real step.
- **Ship, measure, repeat.** Small releases, honest status, no fake progress.
- **Own the stack.** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node, Python, FastAPI, Rust, PostgreSQL, Docker, Cloudflare and self-hosted GPUs.
